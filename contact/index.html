How buying and delivery works

  1. Send your requirement

    Choose the metal, specifications, quantity and delivery location.

  2. Review your quote

    We confirm availability, price and an expected delivery window before you place the order.

  3. Source and verify

    We arrange the material and confirm the agreed grade, weight and test documents before dispatch.

  4. Deliver to your site

    We coordinate transport to your delivery address and follow up through unloading.
