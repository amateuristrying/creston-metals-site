(03) — What metal becomes

From stockyard
to skyline.

  1. Step 01 / 06

    Source the steel

    Graded beams and columns arrive from the stockyard. Each member is matched to the structure it will support.

  2. Step 02 / 06

    Raise the frame

    Columns rise and beams connect them. Together, they form the steel skeleton that carries the building’s weight.

  3. Step 03 / 06

    Build floor by floor

    The frame climbs level by level. More columns and beams extend the structure into a 14-storey tower.

  4. Step 04 / 06

    Enclose the structure

    Floor decks span the frame as glass and façade panels rise around it. The open skeleton becomes an enclosed building.

  5. Step 05 / 06

    Complete the skyline

    The upper structure and crown take their place. The last panels close the building’s outline against the sky.

  6. Step 06 / 06

    Bring it to life

    The lights come on. Steel that began in a stockyard now supports the spaces where people will live and work.

(04) — What metal moves

From coil
to open road.

  1. Step 01 / 06

    Start with a coil

    Rolled steel arrives wound into a coil. This continuous strip is the starting material for the car’s body panels.

  2. Step 02 / 06

    Unwind & level

    The coil pays out through the roller table. The strip is levelled and fed towards the press as flat sheet.

  3. Step 03 / 06

    Press the panels

    Blanks are cut and pressed into shape. Flat steel becomes the curves of a bonnet, roof, doors and body panels.

  4. Step 04 / 06

    Assemble the body

    The formed panels join the body structure. Wheels move into place as the car’s shape comes together.

  5. Step 05 / 06

    Glaze & finish

    Glass is fitted, the body receives its finish and the lamps light up. The assembled steel takes on its final character.

  6. Step 06 / 06

    Take to the road

    The finished car moves into first light. From one continuous strip of steel to the body that carries the journey.
